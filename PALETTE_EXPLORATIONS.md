## Possible hybrid

A common pattern, which might be the real answer:

- **Landing + `/story` + `/rsvp`**: dark-dominant (A or B) — drama and atmosphere
- **`/details` + `/montreal` + `/gifts`**: light-dominant (D) — logistics, reads as stationery, easier to scan

This is where editorial magazines actually live — dark gatefolds for mood, white pages for content. Worth considering but adds design-system work (two complete stylings rather than one + insets).

## Dimensions to react on (use any / many in your reply)

Rather than just picking a letter, it may be more useful to tell me how to turn the dials:

1. **Darkness of base**: warmer (A, C) ↔ blacker/cooler (B)
2. **Warmth of neutrals**: linen/bone/camel (A) ↔ stone/grey (B)
3. **Burgundy intensity**: whisper (A) ↔ muted present (C) ↔ absent (B)
4. **Gilt**: tarnished only (A, C, D) ↔ bright + sparing (B) ↔ both
5. **Ivory temperature**: cream-yellow (C) ↔ neutral-warm (A, D) ↔ nearly-white (B)
6. **Contrast level**: soft (C, D) ↔ crisp (A) ↔ high (B)
7. **Are you open to hybrid (dark moments + light content pages)?**

Or just tell me which mockup block above feels closest and why, and we'll iterate from there.
