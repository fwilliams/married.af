Zoom 1.00× is cover for the box the site actually paints: the frame plus a 40 px bleed on top and sides and 50vh below it for parallax. On an iPhone 100vh is the toolbar-hidden viewport, and the phone frame's box is made exactly that tall, so its crop is device-exact. Horizontal / Vertical are background-position: 0% pins the photo's left/top edge to that box, 100% its right/bottom edge. The gold and dashed rectangles on the small photo are the two crops.

        
On your phone: start the server with bin/serve --host 0.0.0.0, then open this URL with localhost replaced by your Mac's IP. It is a snapshot — after changing sliders, reload the phone with the new URL.
